# Enchanting 2

**Pick your enchantments. See every price. Put everything on one item.**

Enchanting 2 rebuilds enchanting from the ground up: no more random table rolls, no more "Too Expensive!", no more
choosing between Sharpness and Smite. Every vanilla enchantment is still here, most of them go up to level V, and
**49 brand-new enchantments** fill the gaps vanilla left: Night Vision helmets, double jumping, lava walking, 3×3
mining, auto-replanting hoes, shield bashes, and a lot more.

---

## Highlights

- **You choose, the table obeys.** The enchanting table lists every enchantment that fits your item. Pick as many as
  you want, at the level you want, and see the exact XP and lapis cost before you click.
- **No "Too Expensive!"** The anvil has no 40-level cap and never gets pricier the more you use it on an item.
- **Stack everything.** Sharpness *and* Smite. Silk Touch *and* Fortune. All four Protections on one chestplate.
  Mending *and* Infinity. If it fits the item, it goes on.
- **Level V for (almost) everything.** Protection V, Unbreaking V, Fortune V, Looting V, Feather Falling V and more.
- **Simple, honest prices.** 1 level per enchantment level: Sharpness V costs 5. Upgrading only pays for the levels
  you add. Every price is shown before you pay.
- **Move enchantments instead of losing them.** The grindstone can lift a single enchantment off an item onto a book,
  or remove just one curse.
- **Curses pay you back.** Every curse on an item makes enchanting it cheaper.
- **Levels past 30 stop getting more expensive.** Every level after 30 costs the same small amount of XP, with no cap.
- **Hold Ctrl over any item** to see what each of its enchantments actually does.
- **Anvils never break** (you can turn wear back on).
- **Almost everything is adjustable in-game**, from max levels to prices to which enchantments exist at all.

## The enchanting table

- Shows **every** enchantment your item can take, sorted by name. Click to pick, use the arrows to change the level.
- Live totals for XP and lapis, in green when you can afford it and red when you can't.
- Hover any enchantment to see why you can't pick it yet ("Needs 8 bookshelves") or, with Ctrl, what it does.
- Bookshelves still matter: 15 shelves unlock every enchantment's top level, fewer shelves cap the levels.
- Mending and Frost Walker can be bought here, with a full 15 bookshelves.
- Already-enchanted items and books can go back on the table to add more or raise levels.
- Your armor and offhand slots are right in the window, so you can enchant what you're wearing.

## The anvil

- No level cap, no growing "prior work" cost.
- A breakdown beside the slots shows every line of the price and the total.
- A book applies everything it can in one go. Anything your item can't take **stays on the book** instead of vanishing.

## The grindstone

Nothing happens until you choose, so a stray shift-click can never wipe an item.
- **Extract:** move one enchantment from an item onto a plain book. The item keeps everything else.
- **Cleanse:** remove one curse.
- **Disenchant** and **Repair** work like vanilla.

## Better vanilla enchantments

- **Fortune works on far more blocks:** logs, dirt, sand, gravel, clay, snow, leaves, mushroom blocks and more. Blocks
  you placed yourself never count, so there's no infinite place-and-mine trick.
- **Silk Touch picks up mob spawners**, keeping their mob.
- **Infinity on a water or lava bucket** keeps it full forever (well, for 256 uses, and Mending works on it).
- **Axes** take Knockback, Looting and Fire Aspect. **Crossbows** take Flame and Infinity.

## 49 new enchantments

### Helmet
- **Vision**: permanent Night Vision while you wear it, with no flicker.
- **Calm Gaze**: endermen don't get angry when you look at them.
- **Clarity** (I–III): Blindness, Darkness and Nausea wear off faster. Level III makes you immune.
- **Phantom Ward**: phantoms leave you alone, even if you haven't slept.
- **Sustenance** (I–V): you get hungry more slowly, up to 20% at V.

### Chestplate
- **Cushion** (I–III): less damage from flying into walls with an elytra. Also fits elytra.
- **Last Stand** (I–III): a hit that would kill you leaves you at half a heart instead. Recharges over a few minutes,
  with a timer next to your hotbar. Rare.

### Leggings
- **Step Assist**: walk up full blocks without jumping.
- **Anchored** (I–V): get knocked back less when you're hit.
- **Endurance** (I–V): sprinting costs less hunger, up to 25% at V.
- **Sure Footing** (I–III): soul sand, honey and powder snow stop slowing you down.

### Boots
- **Swiftness** (I–V): walk faster.
- **Double Jump**: jump once more in mid-air. It also breaks your fall, and with an elytra the second jump takes off.
- **Lava Walking**: walk on lava like solid ground. Sneak to sink. (Bring Fire Resistance, it still burns.)
- **Snow Walker**: walk on powder snow without sinking or freezing, in any boots.

### Any armor piece
- **Toughness** (I–V): extra armor toughness, so big hits hurt less.
- **Health** (I–V): extra hearts. A full set at level V gives ten more. Rare.

### Elytra
- **Speed** (I–V): fly up to 50% faster, and firework rockets push harder.
- **Flight** (I–V): hold jump while gliding for a rocket-style boost, no rockets needed. Uses elytra durability.

### Shield
- **Deflect** (I–III): arrows and fireballs you block can fly straight back at whoever shot them.
- **Shield Bash** (I–III): attack with your shield up to shove enemies back and slow them.
- **Steadfast** (I–III): axes disable your shield for less time. Level III makes it immune.
- **Spiked** (I–V): Thorns for shields: melee attackers you block get hurt.
- **Bulwark** (I–III): blocking a hit gives you a few extra hearts for 5 seconds.
- **Riposte** (I–V): your next hit after a block does extra damage.
- **Steady Stance**: walk at full speed while holding your shield up.

### Melee weapons (swords, axes, spears, mace, trident)
- **Vampirism** (I–V): heal for part of the damage you deal.
- **Beheading** (I–V): a chance for mobs to drop their heads.
- **Attack Speed** (I–V): swing faster, up to 25% at V.
- **Quick Strike** (I–V): a chance for a hit to land twice.

### Melee weapons and bows
- **Withering** (I–V): hits apply Wither.
- **Poisoning** (I–V): hits apply Poison.
- **Freezing** (I–V): hits slow the target.
- **Critical Strike** (I–V): a chance for +50% damage.

### Bow and crossbow
- **TrueShot Aura**: arrows fly dead straight for 5 seconds before dropping.

### Tools
- **Smelting**: ores, sand and logs drop already smelted, with the furnace XP.
- **Area Mining**: pickaxes and shovels break a 3×3 area. Sneak to mine a single block.
- **Replant** (hoe): harvesting a fully grown crop replants it automatically.
- **Tiller** (hoe, I–III): till a whole patch at once, up to 7×7.

### Tools and weapons
- **Reach** (I–III): reach blocks and mobs from farther away.
- **XP Boost** (I–V): more XP from mining and kills.
- **Magnetic**: drops and XP go straight into your inventory.
- **Ender Link**: drops go straight into your ender chest.

### Anything with durability
- **Keep on Death**: the item stays with you when you die, back in the same slot.

### Curses (each one makes enchanting the item cheaper)
- **Curse of Fragility** (I–V): the item wears out faster.
- **Curse of Weight** (I–V): armor that slows you down.
- **Curse of Greed** (I–V): less XP from kills and mining.
- **Curse of Fumbling** (I–V): mine more slowly.
- **Curse of Hunger** (I–V): armor that makes you hungry faster.

## Make it your own

With [Mod Menu](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/modmenu) and
[YACL](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yacl) installed you get a full settings screen (both optional).
You can:
- turn any enchantment off, or keep it out of the table, loot chests or villager trades,
- change any enchantment's max level (up to 255) and price,
- bring back vanilla's "can't combine" rules for any group,
- set an anvil cost cap, bring back anvil wear, or keep the vanilla XP curve,
- multiply every mob's health (1× to 100×) if you want a real challenge.

On a server, the server's settings apply to everyone and are shown to each player.

## Installing

- **Minecraft** 1.21.11 · **Fabric Loader** 0.19.5+ · **Fabric API** required · Java 21
- **Singleplayer:** drop the jar and Fabric API into your `mods` folder.
- **Multiplayer:** install it on the **server and every player**. Players without the mod get a clear message
  when they try to join. With the mod on a vanilla server, you just get vanilla enchanting.
- Works with recipe viewers (JEI, REI, EMI) and keeps other mods' enchantments intact.

**Adding it to an existing world is safe.** Your items and levels stay as they are.

**Removing it later?** Back up first: the new enchantments disappear from your items (the items themselves stay),
and vanilla enchantments above their normal max keep working.
